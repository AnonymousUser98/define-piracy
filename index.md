# AnonymousUser98's Piracy Definition Page
The term "piracy" usually refers to the ethical side of the issue (e.g. harming developers) as well as the legal side in most cases (e.g. "it's illegal"). However, there are some things that I wouldn't consider piracy even though the DMCA says they're illegal.

**DISCLAIMER:** I'm not a lawyer.

### Piracy vs. Legality Table

🟢 = Legal\
🟡 = Legally Grey\
🔴 = Illegal

If there is a 🏴‍☠️ in the _Piracy_ column, then I would call it piracy.

|Activity|Piracy|Illegal according to the DMCA|
| --- | --- | --- |
|Downloading a ROM of a game you don't own|🏴‍☠️|🔴|
|Hacking your console temporarily for repairs||🟢|
|Hacking your console to install homebrew||🟡|
|Dumping a game you own and playing it on a modded console or emulator||🟡|
|Downloading a ROM of a game you legally own||🔴|
|Patching a legally-dumped ROM to play a ROM hack||🟡|
|Dumping a game and selling the original|🏴‍☠️|🔴|
|Dumping a game and selling the dumped copy|🏴‍☠️|🔴|
|Downloading a console BIOS or a ROM of a free game/app||🔴|
